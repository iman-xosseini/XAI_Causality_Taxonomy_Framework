### a. Explanation of the Failure

Your machine experienced a Heat Dissipation Failure because it couldn't release enough heat while functioning. This happened because the Temperature Difference (the gap between air temperature and machine's internal heat) was only about 7.8 K; it needed to be more than 8.6 K for safe operation. The machine's rotational speed dropped to 1263, below the critical 1380 mark. These lower numbers meant the machine was inefficient at cooling down and handling wear, causing it to overheat and fail. 

---

### b. Actionable Recommendation

To avoid future failures, make sure the coolant systems (MQL systems) are always filled and working well. Check the coolant nozzles regularly to be certain they’re providing proper coverage to the machine. Pay close attention to any heat alerts from the sensors. If temperatures go too high, pause the machine to let it cool down, ensuring safety and preventing breakdowns. These steps will help keep the machine running smoothly and reduce the risk of overheating.
