### a. Explanation of the Failure

The machine failed due to an "Overstrain Failure," which happens when the combination of tool wear and torque becomes too high — in this case, exceeding 11,000. The main reason was the high tool wear level of 186 and its interaction with the torque. The machine's speed was 1,327 RPM, and the power output was substantial at 80,947, contributing to increased tool wear. These factors strained the machine beyond its limits, leading to the failure.

### b. Actionable Recommendation

To prevent future failures, operate the machine within its recommended torque and speed settings. When working with heavy loads, be particularly careful to avoid overstraining the system.
