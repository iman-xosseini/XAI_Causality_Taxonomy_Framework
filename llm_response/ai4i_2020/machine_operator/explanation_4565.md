### a. Explanation of the Failure

The machine experienced a **Heat Dissipation Failure** because it wasn't cooling down enough while running, leading to problems. The temperature difference was too low (only 8.1 K), and the machine was running slower than it should (1275 RPM instead of at least 1380 RPM). This slowed cooling caused the machine to overheat. Rotational speed impacted tool wear significantly (effect strength of 0.65), making the parts wear out faster, further worsening the cooling issue. Essentially, inefficient cooling at lower machine speed caused the failure.

### b. Actionable Recommendation

To avoid future heat issues, ensure the coolant systems (MQL) have enough fluid and are working well. Regularly check the nozzle positions to make sure they're spraying coolant correctly. Keep an eye on the machine's heat sensors. If the temperature gets too high, pause the operation to prevent damage. Regularly performing these checks will help maintain proper machine cooling and avoid overheating.
