### b. Actionable Recommendation

To prevent future power failures, regularly check the machine’s display to monitor power usage. The interface will alert or provide color-coded indicators when loads are high or abnormal. If you see these indicators, reduce machine loads or pause work until conditions normalize. Additionally, pay attention to maintenance alerts related to torque or power levels. Following these steps will help keep the machine running smoothly and avoid power failures.
