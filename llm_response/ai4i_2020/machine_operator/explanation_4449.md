### a. Explanation of the Failure

The Heat Dissipation Failure in your machine happened because the difference between the air temperature and the process temperature was too small (7.9 K), and the rotational speed was too low (1378), below safe levels. Low speeds and small temperature differences can cause overheating because heat can't escape well enough. Tool wear is also involved, creating more heat. These factors together, especially temperature and speed, caused the failure.

---

### b. Actionable Recommendation

To avoid future failures: 

1. Make sure the MQL (Minimum Quantity Lubrication) system always has enough coolant and is working properly. 
2. Regularly check the coolant nozzles to ensure they are correctly positioned for the best cooling.
3. Pay attention to heat alerts from sensors, and if temperatures get too high, stop the machine to let it cool down. 

By doing these steps, you can help prevent overheating and keep the machine running smoothly.
