### a. Explanation of the Failure

Your machine failed due to what we call an "Overstrain Failure." This happened because the combined effect of tool wear and high torque exceeded safe levels, specifically going over 11000. The main causes were the high tool wear value of 194 and a rotational speed of 1335. The rotation speed increases tool wear, which together with high torque caused the wear to torque ratio to exceed safe limits, leading to a failure. In simple terms, the machine was pushed too hard, causing parts to wear out rapidly.

### b. Actionable Recommendation

To keep your machine running smoothly, always stick to the recommended torque and speed settings. Be extra careful and slow down when the machine is working hard or carrying heavy loads. If you notice any unusual vibrations or if the machine signals any alarms, report it to the maintenance team immediately. This way, small issues can be fixed before they turn into big problems.
