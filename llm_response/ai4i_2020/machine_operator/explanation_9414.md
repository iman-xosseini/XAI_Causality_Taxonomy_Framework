### a. Explanation of the Failure

The machine experienced an Overstrain Failure due to a combination of factors. The product type, designated as "Low", and the tool's wear level combined with excessive torque led to a critical condition. The measurement of tool wear and torque reached a value over 11157.3, exceeding the safety threshold of 11000. This largely contributed to the failure. Operating at a rotational speed of 1354, which affects both tool wear and the strain on the machine, further compounded the issues. High tool wear can increase machine strain, causing overstrain.

### b. Actionable Recommendation

To prevent future failures, always keep the machine’s torque and speed within safe limits. Be mindful when the machine is under heavy load and watch for any unusual vibrations or stress signals. If you notice strange vibrations or alarms indicating strain, report these to maintenance immediately. This proactive approach helps maintain the health of the machine and prevents unexpected breakdowns. Proper monitoring and timely reporting will ensure safer and more efficient operations.
