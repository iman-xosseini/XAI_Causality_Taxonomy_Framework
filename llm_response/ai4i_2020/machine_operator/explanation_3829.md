### a. Explanation of the Failure

The machine experienced a Heat Dissipation Failure because the cooling effect was not sufficient. The temperature difference, which shows how much heat was being lost to the surrounding air, fell slightly below the critical threshold of 8.6, hitting 8.6. Also, the machine’s rotational speed, at 1366 RPM, was just under the safe level of 1380 RPM. These conditions, along with some tool wear, caused the machine to overheat. The key factors were the low rotational speed and inadequate cooling, which increase temperature and stress on the machine. 

### b. Actionable Recommendation

To prevent future Heat Dissipation Failures, ensure that the Minimum Quantity Lubrication (MQL) systems always have enough coolant and are working properly. Regularly check that coolant nozzles are properly positioned for optimal cooling. Keep an eye on the machine's temperature sensors; if you see a heat alert, pause operation immediately. These steps will help in maintaining the machine's health and avoiding overheating issues.
