### a. Explanation of the Failure

The machine experienced a Heat Dissipation Failure because the temperature difference between the air and the machine was too low, at just 7.8K when it needed to be over 8.6K to work safely. Also, the machine's spinning speed was below 1380, at only 1263. These conditions meant the machine couldn't cool down properly, leading to overheating. Tool wear and how hard the machine was working made it harder for it to cool down, resulting in the failure. It's crucial to maintain proper heat control to avoid damage.

### b. Actionable Recommendation

To prevent future overheating, regularly ensure that the MQL system is filled with coolant and is working correctly. Check the positions of nozzles to confirm they are directing the coolant where needed. Always keep an eye out for heat alerts from the sensors, and if temperatures go beyond safe levels, immediately pause machine operations. These steps will help keep the machine running smoothly and safely.
