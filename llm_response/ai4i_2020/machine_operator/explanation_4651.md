### a. Explanation of the Failure

The machine experienced a heat dissipation failure because the temperature difference between the air and the process was too low (8.2 K), below the safe threshold of 8.6 K, and the rotational speed was lower than the required 1380 RPM, at 1349 RPM. This condition caused inadequate cooling, leading to overheating. The rotational speed directly affects tool wear, and both these factors influenced each other, worsening the heat condition. Inadequate cooling lowered the system's ability to manage heat, ultimately prompting the failure.

### b. Actionable Recommendation

To avoid future heat issues, ensure the coolant system (MQL) is always filled and operational. Regularly check that coolant nozzles are well-positioned for proper coverage. Stay alert for any sensor warnings about high temperatures. If such alerts appear, pause the machinery to prevent overheating. Following these steps can help maintain optimal machine performance and prevent further failures from heat buildup.
