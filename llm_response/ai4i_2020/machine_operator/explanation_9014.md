### a. Explanation of the Failure

An overstrain failure occurred because the product’s wear-to-torque ratio exceeded 12,000, reaching about 12,689. This is affected by high tool wear and rotational speed. Specifically, your machine was running at a high speed of 1,340 RPM and the tool wear was high at 207. Think of it like using a blunt tool to cut at a high speed—it puts too much strain on the machine, causing it to fail.

---

### b. Actionable Recommendation

To keep the machine running smoothly, stay within recommended torque and speed limits. Be extra careful when using the machine for heavy tasks. If you notice any unusual shaking or if the machine alerts you to any strain, let maintenance know right away. This will help prevent future problems and keep everything operating correctly.
