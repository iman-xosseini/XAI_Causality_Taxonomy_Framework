### a. Explanation of the Failure

The Heat Dissipation Failure happened because the temperature difference between the air and machine was too small (only 7.7 K instead of over 8.6 K). With a low spinning speed of 1329 RPM, the machine couldn’t handle the heat effectively. The main factors include high tool wear (148 units) and the torque-related stress on the tool, which increased due to reduced speed. This combination of low temperature difference and slowing speed led to inefficient cooling and caused the failure.

### b. Actionable Recommendation

To prevent future failures, make sure your coolant system (MQL) is filled and working right. Check the coolant nozzles regularly to ensure they are aimed correctly to cool the machine efficiently. Keep an eye on temperature alerts from your sensors. If things get too hot, stop the operation until temperatures return to safer levels. These steps will help keep your machine running smoothly and avoid overheating.
